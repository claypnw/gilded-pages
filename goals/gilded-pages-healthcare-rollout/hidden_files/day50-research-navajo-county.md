Town: Show Low
Inventoried as: Navajo County Health Department — 600 N 9th Pl, Show Low — (928) 532-6050 — clinic / public health — NEW FIND 2026-11-20
Flag on the inventory: county government public-health office — does it belong in a healthcare business directory like other clinics?

## Verdict

**PUBLISHABLE as a directory entry — with the note, stated in the entry itself, that it is a county public-health office (the Navajo County Public Health Services District, Show Low Office), not a private clinic or walk-in primary-care practice.**

Reason: it is not an administrative office only. At this exact address and phone it delivers direct healthcare to the public by appointment — immunizations, HIV/STI and TB testing and treatment, family planning / well-woman exams, WIC, and overdose-prevention / Narcan training — on a sliding scale, accepting Medicaid and private insurance (CDC NPIN record). That is the same public-facing clinic function other Gilded Pages clinic entries perform. Excluding it would omit a real, low-cost source of care in Show Low. The directory note must set expectations: services are program-based and by appointment, hours/program availability vary by location, and the county itself says to call ahead to verify services.

## Services offered to the public (sourced)

From the county's own site:
- **Family planning / reproductive health** — well-woman exams, Pap smear, breast exam, birth control, STD testing and treatment, nurse follow-up every 3 months; for uninsured, under-insured, or insured females; Title V-eligible clients at no cost, $30 cash-pay annual well-woman exam for others, insurance billed where applicable; 340B office (low-cost medication). Show Low clinics twice a month with Jaskiranjit Hanson, NP, CNM — call 928-532-6050 for an appointment. (County Family Planning page)
- **WIC** (Women, Infants, and Children) — nutrition education, breastfeeding support, supplemental foods, referrals; Show Low WIC listed at this same address and phone, hours Mon–Thu 7:00 AM–5:30 PM. (WICPrograms directory listing for Show Low; county WIC program is part of NCPHSD)
- **Overdose prevention** — Narcan training and unused-medication drop-box list, at 600 N 9th Pl, (928) 532-6050 ext. 5522. (211 Arizona listing of the county's Overdose Prevention Program)
- District-wide programs the county / directories tie to this department: immunizations, environmental health inspections, vital records (birth certificates), chronic disease self-management. (CountyOffice department profile; county site program pages for Immunizations and Vital Records exist under Public Health Services)

- Testing: chlamydia, gonorrhea, syphilis, conventional HIV, hepatitis B, hepatitis C, TB
- Treatment: STI treatment, TB treatment, TB care on-site by appointment
- Vaccines: adult hepatitis B, hepatitis A, HPV

From Arizona DHS immunization referral material: Navajo County Public Health Services District is listed as a county health department clinic authorized to vaccinate underinsured children with VFC (Vaccines for Children) vaccine — supporting immunizations as a public service of the district (the Show Low line in that older document carries this same phone, 928-532-6050; its street line there is garbled in extraction, so the address is taken from the county's own current page, not from that PDF).

## Flag resolution

The flag asked whether a county government office belongs alongside clinics. Finding: **yes, publish it, labeled plainly as a county public-health office.** It holds public clinic sessions, tests and treats patients, gives vaccines, and takes appointments from the general public at a published address, phone, and hours — directory-useful healthcare, especially for uninsured / under-insured readers (sliding scale, Medicaid, no-cost Title V and VFC programs). It is not a private business, and the entry must not imply walk-in primary care: add the note "County public-health office — program clinics by appointment; call ahead to verify services."
