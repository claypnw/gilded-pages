### History / why it most likely is not operating (context, NOT proof of closure)
The same court opinion records, in detail (pp.1, 4 of the opinion, URL above):
- In 2019 the Medicare program-integrity contractor Qlarant reviewed a sample of 42 of the agency's claims, denied 23, and extrapolated an overpayment of **$3,974,669**.
- On redetermination (2020-08-20) the Medicare Administrative Contractor reduced that to **$2,821,653.60** and demanded payment by **2021-02-18**; the agency's request for an ALJ hearing (filed 2020-12-23) was still pending when the court ruled.
- The court **dismissed the agency's suit for lack of jurisdiction** (it had not exhausted administrative remedies) and denied its preliminary injunction as moot (Order, p.1).
- The opinion states **approximately 98 percent of the agency's total annual revenue derived from Medicare reimbursement** — i.e., a $2.82M recoupment against a Medicare-dependent agency in 2021.
This is strong context for disappearance, but it is **not** a closure record: no source found states that the agency closed, was terminated from Medicare, or dissolved. The verdict is therefore HELD/unverifiable, not "closed" — the same standard the rollout applied to Apria Show Low and Summit Eye Surgeons (leaning closed, never counted closed without a source).
