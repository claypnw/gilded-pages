**Recommendation: HELD for a phone check.** Call (928) 646-0347 first; if no answer / disconnected / reaches another business, call the Phoenix number (602) 362-5100 and ask whether the Cottonwood clinic at 1760 E Villa Dr Suite A still sees clients, and its hours.
